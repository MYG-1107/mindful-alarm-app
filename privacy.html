Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Compliant with EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679)

1. Data Protection Overview

ZenTimer is built with a privacy-first architecture. We do not collect, process, sell, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII) to external servers or third-party advertisers.

2. Local Device Storage (`localStorage`)

To provide a seamless experience, ZenTimer stores your preferred application settings (such as chime preferences, voice toggles, and cookie consent states) directly inside your web browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

3. Browser Permissions

ZenTimer requests explicit permissions for specific device capabilities:

  • Notifications API: Used solely to alert you when a meditation timer finishes if the browser is in another tab.
  • Screen Wake Lock API: Used to prevent your mobile or desktop screen from dimming during an active session.
  • Audio Session Playback: Used to maintain background execution while your mobile phone screen is locked.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR

As an EU resident, you have the right to access, rectify, or clear any local storage data created by this site. You can clear all app preferences at any time by clearing your browser cache or clicking "Cookie & Data Preferences" on the homepage.

5. Contact & Data Controller

For questions regarding this privacy policy or data compliance, contact us via our GitHub repository issuer.
