Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

1. Local-first processing

ZenTimer stores routine presets, session history, streak summaries and custom audio in browser storage on your device. This application does not upload those records to a ZenTimer server.

2. No advertising analytics

The application does not include third-party advertising or analytics scripts. The PWA/service worker caches app files locally for offline use.

3. Optional browser permissions

Notifications are used only after you grant browser permission. Screen Wake Lock is requested while a session is running when supported. Audio playback is controlled by your browser's media policies.

4. Local data controls

You can use Temporary Mode to avoid persistence. You can also clear session history from the app or clear the site's browser storage through your browser settings.

5. Third-party hosting

When hosted on GitHub Pages or another provider, standard infrastructure logs may be handled by that host according to its own policies. Those logs are outside this application's local data model.
